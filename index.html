FAQ

Questions

Is Clara really free?

Yes. Completely free. No trials, no premium tiers, no credit card required. We believe time protection shouldn't be a luxury.

How is Clara different from other calendar apps?

Other apps show your schedule. Clara diagnoses your overload and tells you what to do about it. She's not a calendar—she's your time protection agent.

How does health data integration work?

Clara connects to Apple Health to read sleep, steps, HRV, and activity. This data informs your capacity calculation—bad sleep = reduced capacity = Clara protects you from overcommitting.

Which calendars does Clara support?

Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, and Apple Calendar. You can connect multiple accounts from each provider.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use OAuth for calendar connections (we never see your passwords). You can delete all your data with one tap at any time.

Clara is currently iOS-only. Android is in development—join our waitlist to be notified.
